    How fights work

    Fast attack: a normal hit. Strong attack: hits much harder but always goes last. Block: take less damage (IQ makes it stronger). Dodge: avoids Heavy Swings completely, but not Quick Hooks. You can’t use the same move twice in a row. Speed decides who moves first, Strength hits harder, Stamina adds HP, Focus gives a chance of a big hit, and IQ Level 5 shows the opponent’s next moves.
